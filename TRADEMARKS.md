# Redunar branding policy

The Redunar name, REDUNAR wordmark, and Comet R logo identify the upstream
Redunar project. This policy explains how to refer to the project and distinguish
official releases from independent products.

No trademark registration is claimed by this policy. It does not create
registered trademark rights or guarantee protection in any jurisdiction.

## Software and artwork licenses

Redunar remains licensed under [GPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE). You may use, modify,
fork, redistribute, and sell the software under that license. This policy does
not add conditions to those GPL permissions, revoke existing copyright licenses
for artwork, or change third-party licenses and notices.

The branding expectations below concern product identity and representations of
affiliation. They do not restrict uses permitted by applicable law, including
truthful descriptive references, attribution, criticism, and review.

## References and community use

You may use the Redunar name to discuss, review, compare, or link to Redunar.
You may reproduce screenshots and the logo to identify the project accurately.
For references to the official project, use the supplied logo without changing
its design; proportional resizing is fine.

Describe independent work accurately, for example, "based on Redunar" or
"an unofficial package of Redunar." Do not imply project approval, sponsorship,
partnership, or official support without written permission.

## Redistribution and packaging

You may redistribute unmodified upstream releases, or builds of unmodified
upstream source, with their existing branding, free of charge or for a fee,
while complying with the GPL. Clearly identify your distribution or build and
who provides support. Do not describe your own build as an official upstream
release or present your signatures as upstream signatures.

Routine packaging adjustments, such as installation paths and dependency
metadata, may retain the branding when disclosed and when they do not materially
change application behavior or misrepresent its origin.

## Modified products and forks

For independently distributed products with materially changed functionality,
privacy, update behavior, or paid feature variants, use a distinct product name
and logo unless you have written branding permission. You may still describe the
product as based on Redunar and must preserve required copyright and license
notices.

Keeping upstream names in source history or in a clearly identified repository
fork is fine. This does not make that fork or its releases official Redunar.
You do not need branding permission to make a private fork or to publish a
GPL-compliant fork under distinct branding.

## Avoid misleading branding

Do not use Redunar branding in product names, domains, accounts, store listings,
or promotional material in a way that is likely to confuse people about origin
or endorsement. Names such as "Redunar Pro" or "Redunar Premium" for an
independently modified product need written branding permission, unless the use
is otherwise permitted by law.

These expectations apply to individuals and organizations alike. They do not
prohibit commercial use or properly distinguished GPL-compliant commercial forks.

## Official channels and permission requests

The upstream project is at [github.com/shukzi/Redunar](https://github.com/shukzi/Redunar)
and [redunar.com](https://redunar.com/).

For branding questions or additional permission, open an issue through the
[feature-suggestion form](https://github.com/shukzi/Redunar/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
Describe the proposed branding, where it will appear, and whether the software
is modified. Only explicit written approval from the project maintainer grants
additional branding permission; submitting a request is not approval.
